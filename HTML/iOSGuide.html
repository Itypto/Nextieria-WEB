Requirements

  • An iOS device
  • A PC
  • Sideloadly set up
  • Don't know how to set up Sideloadly? Click here
  • Developer Mode turned on on the iOS device
  • Don't know how to turn on Developer Mode? Click here
  • 5 GB of free usable storage
  • Device used in tutorial: iPhone XS Max

Step 1

Go to the downloads channel in Discord and click "Here" under "Download here".

Step 2

On the web page, click the yellow "DOWNLOAD NEXTIERIA" button.

Step 3

Read the notice and click "I UNDERSTAND".

Step 4

Select "MOBILE" as your platform.

Step 5

Select "iOS/iPadOS" as your platform.

Step 6

Save the Nextieria-Fortnite.ipa file to your computer.

Step 7

Open Sideloadly, load the Nextieria-Fortnite.ipa file, connect your device, and enter your Apple ID.

Step 8

Click "Start" to begin sideloading the app to your device.

Step 9

In Settings, tap "General" and then select "VPN & Device Management".

Step 10

Under Developer App, select your developer profile.

Step 11

Tap "Trust" for your Apple ID to authorize the app on your device.

Step 12

Open Nextieria and wait for the game data to download.

Step 13

When the welcome screen appears, tap "Login".

Step 14

Go to the Discord downloads channel and tap "Here" under "Mobile login info".

Step 15

Copy the email address and password provided on the Nextieria login credentials page.

Step 16

Return to the login screen, paste your credentials, and tap "LOG IN".

Step 17

Review the End User License Agreement and click "Accept".

Step 18

You are now logged into the lobby and ready to play!
