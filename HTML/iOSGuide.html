Requirements

  • An iOS device
  • A PC
  • Sideloadly set up
  • Don't know how to set up Sideloadly? Click here
  • Developer Mode turned on on the iOS device
  • Don't know how to turn on Developer Mode? Click here
  • 5 GB of free usable storage
  • Device used in tutorial: iPhone XS Max

Step 1

Go to the downloads channel in Discord and click "Here" under iOS to download the IPA.

Step 2

Open Sideloadly, select the Nextieria IPA, connect your iOS device, enter your Apple ID, and click "Start".

Step 3

If the app says "Untrusted Developer", open Settings.

Step 4

In Settings, tap "General".

Step 5

Scroll down and tap "VPN & Device Management".

Step 6

Under Developer App, tap your Apple ID.

Step 7

Tap "Trust" for your Apple ID.

Step 8

Tap "Allow" to confirm that you trust the developer.

Step 9

Open Nextieria and wait for the game data to download.

Step 10

When the welcome screen appears, tap "LOGIN".

Step 11

Select "Epic Games" as your login method.

Step 12

Once you reach this screen close the app and go back to discord

Step 13

Go to the Discord downloads channel and tap "Here" under "Get your mobile login info".

Step 14

Copy the email address and password shown on the Nextieria login info page.

Step 15

Return to the login screen, enter the email and password, and tap "LOG IN".

Step 16

Read the End User License Agreement and tap "ACCEPT".

Step 17

You are now ready to play.
