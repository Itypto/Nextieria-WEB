Nextieria Creator Requirements

Created: September 13, 2026

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

Requirements

Invite link must be in the description or comment section of the video.

All videos must be made by the creator themselves, and not copied from other creators.

Videos must have a minimum length of 5 seconds.

Video must contain Nextieria's watermark.

If you don't follow these requirements, you may be refused from the creator program.

Rewards:

Shorts/Tiktok videos:

  • 1500 views: 1 skin of your choice
  • 3000 views: 1 skin of your choice and 1000 V-Bucks
  • 5000 views: 2 skin of your choice and 1000 V-Bucks
  • 10000 views: 5 skin of your choice and 2000 V-Bucks
  • 20000 or more views: Full Locker

Long videos:

  • 500 views: 1 skin of your choice
  • 1500 views: 1 skin of your choice and 1000 V-Bucks
  • 4000 views: 2 skin of your choice and 1000 V-Bucks
  • 7000 views: 5 skin of your choice and 2000 V-Bucks
  • 12000 or more views: Full Locker
