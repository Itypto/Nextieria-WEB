Issues/problems

Here you can find fixes for common issues/problems. If you cannot find a soulution for your problem please report it in the discord.

Common issues

1. Playlist/news/content not loading in at startup

If the playlist, news, or content is not loading at startup, try the following steps:

  • Go out and in on the playlist selector.
  • Go into a match and then back out.
  • If the above does not work, log out and log back in.
    • Note: we are aware of this issue and are working on a fix for it.
