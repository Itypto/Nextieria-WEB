Requirements

  • An Android device
  • 5 GB of free usable storage
  • Device used in tutorial: Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra

Step 1

Go to the downloads channel in Discord and tap "Here" under "Download here".

Step 2

On the web page, tap the yellow "DOWNLOAD NEXTIERIA" button.

Step 3

Read the notice and tap "I UNDERSTAND".

Step 4

Select "MOBILE" as your platform.

Step 5

Select "ANDROID" as your OS.

Step 6

Wait for the APK file to download.

Step 7

Once the download completes, tap "Open" in the pop-up notification.

Step 8

Tap "Install" to install Nextieria-Fortnite.

Step 9

Once installed, tap "Open" to launch the game app.

Step 10

Wait for the in-game assets (2.36 GB) to download.

Step 11

On the splash screen, tap the blue "Login" button.

Step 12

Switch back to Discord and go to the downloads channel.

Step 13

Tap "Here" under "Mobile login info" to get your credentials.

Step 14

Tap "COPY" next to your email address and password.

Step 15

Return to the game, enter your login details, and tap "LOG IN".

Step 16

Read the End User License Agreement and tap "Accept".

Step 17

You are now in the lobby and ready to play!
