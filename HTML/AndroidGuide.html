Requirements

  • An Android device
  • 5 GB of free usable storage
  • Device used in tutorial: Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra

Step 1

Go to the downloads channel in Discord and tap "Here" under Android.

Step 2

Select your Google account and tap "OK".

Step 3

Tap "Install" to begin.

Step 4

Wait for the game data (3.87 GB) to download.

Step 5

On the welcome screen, tap the green "LOGIN" button.

Step 6

Select "Epic Games" as login method.

Step 7

Leave this screen open and go back to the Discord downloads channel.

Step 8

Tap the link to "Get your mobile login info".

Step 9

Copy the email and password shown on the screen.

Step 10

Return to the login screen, paste your info, and tap "LOG IN".

Step 11

Read the EULA and tap "ACCEPT".

Step 12

You are now ready to play!
